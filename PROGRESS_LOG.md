  - Muscle control artists = joint protection: same neural precision that enables BW3–BW4 wave control protects joints under load
  - The MNN Training Principle: "route through MedPec to [Pec.S], not through Axil to [Dlt.A]" replaces vague "feel the chest" cues
  - BodWave Scale as progression: BW1 (group flex) → BW2 (isolate heads, enough for joint-safe training) → BW3/BW4 (sequential/wave)
  - Joint Protection Table: 7 exercises mapped with target pathway vs common compensation vs joint at risk:

    - Lateral Raise: [Dlt.L+++] → Axil vs [Trp.U+++] → CNXI shrug = AC joint
