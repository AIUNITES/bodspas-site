### Press Page Content

#### Section 1: What Bodybuilders Use Now
- Detailed breakdown of every existing method:
  - Sets × Reps × Weight — tracks load only, nothing about muscles or nerves
  - Muscle Group Names — "chest day" is like "I played some notes"
  - RPE/RIR — subjective effort, can't tell which pathway failed
  - Tempo (3-1-2-0) — closest to MNN but speed only
  - EMG (%MVC) — real lab data but not a notation system, requires electrodes
  - Anatomy Textbooks — data exists but buried in 1,500-page paragraph form
- Comparison table: MNN is the only system checking all boxes (load, effort, individual muscles, nerve mapping, symbolic notation)
- Side-by-side: current gym language vs MNN for same bench press

#### Section 2: Muscle Focus — More Gains, Less Joint Damage
- The Wrong Pathway Problem explained neurally
- Shoulder-dominant bench vs pec-dominant bench in MNN notation (side-by-side cards)
- EMG research on internal focus of attention (20–30%+ activation increase)
- Connection: muscle control wave artists = joint protection skill (same neural precision)
- MNN Training Principle: specific nerve routing replaces vague "feel the chest"
- BodWave Scale as training progression for when muscle focus becomes real
- Joint Protection Table — 7 exercises:
  - Bench: MedPec target vs Axil compensation → shoulder damage
  - OHP: Axil target vs CNXI compensation → cervical spine
  - Row: ThDors target vs MusCut compensation → elbow
  - Squat: Fem/InfGlu target vs DorsRami compensation → lumbar disc
  - Deadlift: InfGlu/Sci.T target vs DorsRami compensation → lumbar
  - Curl: MusCut target vs Axil compensation → shoulder
  - Lat Raise: Axil target vs CNXI compensation → AC joint
